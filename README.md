## Entry

> **ghosting**

## Why this word works

Everyone's Wi-Fi has ghosted them: full bars one second, gone the next — right in the middle of a video call, a download, or the final boss fight. "Ghosting" borrows the dating word for someone who vanishes mid-conversation without explanation, and that is exactly how home Wi-Fi behaves: present and promising until the moment it matters, then silent, with no apology and no reason. It is funny at once because the comparison is instantly recognisable, it is true because every household has lived it, and it is fresh because it dodges the obvious "slow" and "buffering" and instead names the betrayal — the connection that leaves you on read, then comes crawling back the second you give up and switch to mobile data. As a network name it needs no context: one glance and you know this router cannot be trusted.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry word, exactly and with no trailing newline, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
